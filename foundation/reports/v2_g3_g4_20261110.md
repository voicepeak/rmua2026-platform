# G3/G4 status after the 2026-11-10 stage runs

## G3 wind and degradation

- The stage-2/3 probe scripts include the native stage initialization path that
  generates wind/obstacle data, but the runs were NullRHI, truth-controlled,
  all-stop GDB traces aimed at detection components and score.
- No natural wind-zone trajectory, W01 comparison, natural force-zone boundary
  or natural F01/F02 schedule was captured in this round.
- Existing controlled evidence remains: 18/18 injected keyframes, six-face drag
  agreement, forced 0.25/1.0 scaling, controlled W02/F01/F02.
- **G3 remains PARTIAL / UNVERIFIED for natural wind and degradation.**  Do not
  convert the stage-injection wind generation into a natural-wind pass.

## G4 official throughput

- No same-operation official batch throughput was obtained.  The official
  process advances near real time (recorded factor about 1.0000–1.0004), so
  local env-command rates cannot be converted into an official 64/256 ratio.
- Local batch, dense-map, sweep and scenario benchmarks are unchanged.
- **G4 official throughput is explicitly unavailable in this round.**  It remains
  PARTIAL, not a measured official ratio.
